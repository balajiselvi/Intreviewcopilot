2. Top Interview Questions
Q1. Tell me about yourself.
Answer: Focus on business outcomes, leadership, S/4HANA transformation experience, SAP Security architecture and stakeholder management.
Q2. Explain SAP RBAC.
Answer: RBAC assigns access through business roles aligned to job functions while enforcing least privilege and segregation of duties.
Q3. Difference between Composite and Derived Roles.
Answer: Composite roles group business roles. Derived roles inherit menus/authorizations from a master role while varying organizational values.
Q4. What is SU24?
Answer: SU24 stores authorization proposals used by PFCG during role maintenance to improve consistency and reduce manual authorization maintenance.
Q5. Explain SoD.
Answer: Segregation of Duties prevents a single user from performing conflicting business functions such as vendor creation and payment execution.
3. S/4HANA Security Lifecycle
Discover: Define security strategy, governance and scope.
Explore: Identify personas and RBAC model.
Realize: Build roles, Fiori catalogs, testing and GRC integration.
Deploy: User provisioning, cutover, firefighter setup.
Run: Access reviews, SoD monitoring, audit support and continuous improvement.
4. SAP GRC Access Control
ARA: Analyze access risks and SoD.
ARM: Manage access requests through workflow.
EAM: Control emergency access with firefighter IDs and log review.
BRM: Govern business role lifecycle.
5. Fiori Security
Frontend roles provide catalogs, spaces, pages and target mappings.
Backend roles enforce business authorization objects.
Validate OData services, ICF nodes, launchpad configuration and CDS authorizations.
6. Manufacturing Interview Scenarios
Scenario: Production planner cannot release production orders after go-live.
Approach:
1. Reproduce issue.
2. Run SU53/STAUTHTRACE.
3. Validate backend role.
4. Check Fiori catalog/OData.
5. Verify organizational values.
6. Retest and document RCA.
