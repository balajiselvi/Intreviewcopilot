# ISO/IEC 27001 and SAP Security Evidence

## Overview

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 is the requirements standard for an information security management system (ISMS). Edition 3 was published in October 2022 and has amendment ISO/IEC 27001:2022/Amd 1:2024. The standard requires an organisation to establish, implement, maintain, and continually improve an ISMS that manages information-security risk. Conformity means the ISMS meets the standard’s requirements. Certification is a separate decision performed by an accredited certification body and is not created by configuring SAP. Source checked 2026-10-21: ISO official page for ISO/IEC 27001:2022, https://committee.iso.org/standard/27001. This file does not reproduce proprietary Annex A control text.

## Interview Summary

I would explain how SAP access controls and GRC evidence support risk treatment inside an ISMS, and I would refuse to treat a PFCG role or GRC workflow as ISO certification.

## Support an ISO 27001 ISMS with SAP security controls

I would confirm the ISMS scope and policy that include the SAP landscape, then review the risk assessment for access and identity risks on those systems. I would assign risk-treatment measures that SAP Security and GRC can maintain or evidence, such as role configuration, privileged access, logging, change control, and supplier access, and I would maintain a Statement of Applicability that justifies those choices. I would use approved role configuration, access-request workflow history, SoD analysis and mitigation, Firefighter review, transport records, and critical-authorization monitoring as evidence. I would validate the configuration by tracing one treated risk to the SAP control, the retained log or workflow evidence, the owner, and the review cadence. Configuring a control is not proof of ISO/IEC 27001 certification. Source checked 2026-10-21: ISO/IEC 27001:2022 official summary on committee.iso.org; organisation-level ISMS practice without copying Annex A wording.

## Distinguish ISMS conformity from certification

I would say conformity means the organisation’s ISMS meets ISO/IEC 27001 requirements. Certification means an accredited body has assessed that ISMS and issued a certificate to a stated version such as ISO/IEC 27001:2022. An SAP setting can support a treated risk, but it does not by itself establish conformity or certification. Source checked 2026-10-21: ISO official ISO/IEC 27001:2022 page.
