Chapter 6 – MSMP & BRF+ (Workflow Engine Deep Dive) 
 
Q68. What is MSMP? 
Interviewer's Objective 
The interviewer wants to determine whether you understand the workflow 
architecture behind SAP GRC rather than simply approving requests. 
 
Executive Answer 
MSMP (Multi-Step Multi-Process Workflow) is the workflow engine used by SAP 
GRC Access Control to automate approval processes. 
It determines: 
• Who approves the request  
• In what order approvals occur  
• Parallel or sequential approvals  
• Escalation rules  
• Notifications  
• Workflow completion  
MSMP separates workflow logic from business logic, making workflows 
configurable rather than hardcoded. 
 
Business Benefits 
MSMP provides: 
• Standardized approvals  
• Workflow automation  
• Audit trail  
• Flexible routing  
• Escalation management  
• Compliance

Q73. What is an Agent? 
Executive Answer 
An Agent is the person or group responsible for approving a workflow stage. 
Examples: 
• Manager  
• Role Owner  
• Security Administrator  
• Compliance Officer  
• Firefighter Owner  

Agents can be determined: 
• Statically  
• Dynamically using BRF+  
• From HR organizational data  
• Through organizational rules  
 
Q74. Explain BRF+. 
This is one of the most frequently asked technical questions. 
 
Executive Answer 
BRF+ (Business Rule Framework Plus) is SAP's rule engine used to make workflow 
decisions. 
MSMP controls how the workflow runs. 
BRF+ decides where it goes. 
BRF+ determines: 
• Approvers  
• Paths  
• Notifications  
• Escalations  
• Conditional routing  
 
Example 
IF Company Code = 1000 
 
↓ 
 
Finance Manager 
 
ELSE 
 
Finance Director 

No ABAP coding is required.

When to Use

• High-risk requests  
• Financial access  
• Production access  
Parallel: 
• Low-risk requests  
• Faster turnaround  
• Independent approvals

Q82. Real Interview Scenario 
Interviewer 
"A Production Access Request is waiting for approval. The manager has left the 
company. What would you do?" 
Executive Answer 
I would first confirm where the request is waiting within the MSMP workflow and 
verify why the approval has not progressed. I would then review how the approver is 
determined—through HR hierarchy, Role Owner assignment, or BRF+ rules. 

If the manager has left the organization, I would work with HR or the business to 
identify the officially designated replacement or delegate rather than manually 
bypassing the workflow. If delegation or substitution is already configured, I would 
verify that it is functioning correctly. After resolving the immediate request, I would 
update the organizational data or BRF+ rules to ensure future requests are routed 
to the correct approver. 
This preserves governance while avoiding recurring workflow failures. 
 
Q83. Real Manufacturing Scenario 
Interviewer 
"During an S/4HANA go-live weekend, hundreds of access requests are delayed 
because the approval workflow is too long. Business leaders ask you to remove 
several approval steps temporarily. What would you recommend?" 
Executive Answer 
I would avoid removing governance controls without assessing the associated risk. 
My approach would be: 
1. Categorize requests by risk level.  
2. Fast-track low-risk requests through an approved simplified workflow.  
3. Retain full approvals for high-risk or production-critical access.  
4. Enable temporary parallel approvals where appropriate to reduce 
turnaround time.  
5. Ensure ARA continues to run before provisioning.  
6. Monitor workflow volumes throughout the go-live period and restore the 
standard workflow once hypercare is complete.  
This approach maintains compliance while supporting business continuity during a 
critical deployment. 
 
Senior Consultant Tips 
When discussing MSMP and BRF+: 
• Emphasize workflow governance, not just configuration.  
• Explain how BRF+ enables dynamic routing without custom ABAP.  

• Highlight business continuity through escalation and delegation.  
• Demonstrate a structured troubleshooting methodology for workflow 
issues.  
• Connect your answers to S/4HANA transformation and manufacturing go-
live scenarios, where workflow efficiency is critical.
